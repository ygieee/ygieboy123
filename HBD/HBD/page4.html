Notes📜

Simple notes to read when you're feeling down 💖

✨ Pray every day and trust God’s perfect timing.

“For I know the plans I have for you, declares the Lord, plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future.” – Jeremiah 29:11

✨ Smile and find joy in small things every day.

“A cheerful heart is good medicine.” – Proverbs 17:22

✨ Don’t be afraid; God is with you in every problem.

“Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid; the Lord is with you.” – Joshua 1:9

✨ Cast your worries on Him and find peace.

“Cast all your anxiety on him because he cares for you.” – 1 Peter 5:7

✨ Rest when you are tired; your body and soul need it.

“Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest.” – Matthew 11:28

✨ Love yourself as God loves you; you are precious.

“I praise you because I am fearfully and wonderfully made.” – Psalm 139:14
